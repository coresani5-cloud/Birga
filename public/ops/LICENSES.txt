Birga Strike — 3D assetlar va litsenziyalar (public/ops/)

  Muallif:    Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)
  Litsenziya: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Manba:      https://poly.pizza/m/PpLF4rt4ah (model sahifasida "Public Domain (CC0)")
              olingan nusxa: npm @netforge/asset-library-files@0.2.0 → library/lowpoly/soldier.glb
              (sha256 a462f808c29ac6e20fed9d0713990c3edf6f6e61f66f3476d35a2ffb2935d93b)
  O'zgarishlar: meshopt siqish ochildi; AK dan boshqa 13 ta qurol olib tashlandi;
              animatsiyalar: Idle→idle, Run_Gun→run, Idle_Shoot→shoot, HitReact→hit, Death→death.
  O'yinda shu fayldagi AK modeli ishlatiladi: o'yinchi qo'lidagi qurol (first-person) va askarlar qo'lida.
